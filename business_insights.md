#  Business Insights

## Product Performance

The analysis identifies the products generating the highest total sales.
High-performing products should receive continued marketing and inventory attention.

## Regional Performance

Regional analysis helps identify stronger and weaker markets.
Lower-performing regions can be targeted with focused marketing strategies.

## Revenue Drivers

Total sales are influenced by both quantity sold and unit price.
Increasing sales volume while maintaining an effective pricing strategy can improve revenue.

## High-Value Orders

High-value orders can be identified using SQL filters.
These orders may represent important customers or valuable sales opportunities.

## Customer Analysis

Customer and regional analysis helps understand where sales are being generated
and supports better customer-focused business decisions.

## Advanced SQL

CTEs, subqueries, JOINs, and window functions were used to perform advanced analysis
and extract meaningful information from the sales data.

## Conclusion

SQL can transform raw business data into useful insights that support
data-driven decision-making.
